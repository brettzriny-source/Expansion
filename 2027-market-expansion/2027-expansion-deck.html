The policy-offset map — re-plotted on live Panorama data

Out-of-pocket is the outcome, not the axis

Every market sits at its install cost (vertical) against the rebate stack it can reach (horizontal). The diagonals are what a homeowner actually pays — net out-of-pocket. The 11 amber markets are our 2027 candidates: we want them landing down and to the right, where the stack does the heavy lifting.

Sources & caveats. Rebate stack (x) = maximum stackable rebate (utility + state + federal HEAR where live/approved/pending), pulled live from Panorama rebates.js (project-panorama, monorepo conductor-playground) — data basis April 2026, files last synced 2026-08-24.  Install cost (y) is MODELED, not a Panorama field — Panorama stores no per-market install price; y = $19k national base × regional cost factor (from Panorama warehouseCost) + cold-climate adder (from heating degree-days). Treat the vertical axis as directional, not quoted pricing.  Amber open rings (Raleigh-Durham, Austin, Charlotte, Las Vegas, Richmond) are estimated — these 5 have no Panorama record; rebate figures are inferred from each state’s HEAR framework and the nearest Panorama analog.  Federal 25C credit expired Dec 31 2025 and is excluded. Markets with rebate > $15k are clamped to the right edge. Vancouver omitted (no rebate record).
