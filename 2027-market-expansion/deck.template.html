The policy-offset map — re-plotted on live Panorama data

Out-of-pocket is the outcome, not the axis

Each market sits at its install cost (vertical) against the market-rate rebate stack (horizontal) — utility + state only, with income-qualified HEAR stripped out because it doesn’t reach our buyers. The diagonals are net out-of-pocket. Without HEAR the stack is thin almost everywhere, so OOP runs $12–23k: the 12 amber markets are our 2027 candidates, and the map shows which keep out-of-pocket lowest (down-right) versus which lean on policy market-rate customers can’t use (up-left).

Sources & caveats. Rebate stack (x) = market-rate stack = utility + state programs available without income qualification, pulled live from Panorama rebates.js (project-panorama, monorepo conductor-playground). Federal HEAR (HEEHRA) is excluded — it is income-capped (≤150% AMI) and does not reach market-rate buyers; Panorama bakes an $8k HEAR line into its full stack, which we subtract where it applies. The market-rate 25C credit expired Dec 31 2025 (also excluded). Data basis April 2026, files last synced 2026-08-24.  Install cost (y) is MODELED, not a Panorama field — Panorama stores no per-market install price; y = $19k national base × regional cost factor (from Panorama warehouseCost) + cold-climate adder (heating degree-days). Treat the vertical axis as directional, not quoted pricing.  Amber open rings (Raleigh-Durham, Austin, Charlotte, Las Vegas, Richmond) are estimated — no Panorama record; rebate inferred from each state’s programs and the nearest Panorama analog.  Diagonal bands mark net out-of-pocket at $12k/$16k/$20k. Markets whose market-rate stack exceeds $12k clamp to the right edge. Vancouver omitted (no rebate record).
