Beyond the Numbers

+Brand presence. Wrapped trucks are rolling billboards at every job site and driveway.
+Brand control at the curb. No risk of a tech pulling up in a dirty, damaged, lifted/modified, or sticker-covered personal vehicle — every truck presents one clean, professional image in the customer's driveway.
+Recruiting & retention. A company truck is a real perk; techs stop putting wear on their own cars.
+Tool security & readiness. Standardized, locked upfit means every truck is job-ready.
+Telematics & control. Routing, safety, and utilization data you can't get from personal cars.
−Fleet overhead. Titling, registration, downtime, and admin the personal-vehicle model avoids.
−Utilization risk. Fixed vehicle cost is largely sunk whether or not the truck is driven.
