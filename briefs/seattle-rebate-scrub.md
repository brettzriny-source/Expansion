# Seattle rebate scrub — working note

*Internal. Not published in the Compass artifact.*
*Scrubbed 2026-09-01 against Jetson's Heat Pump Rebate Programs database in Notion (last validated 2026-07-10).*

## What the card carries

**$600** — Seattle City Light Midstream HVAC+. It is the only program that is
simultaneously open to a market-rate customer, valid for a gas-furnace
conversion, in a territory Seattle customers are actually in, and one we can
use today without registering.

## Why the richer programs are not in that number

Three separate disqualifiers. A program only counts if it clears all three.

**1 — Different utility territory.** A customer sits in exactly one electric
territory, so these never stack with SCL. Same rule the brief already applies
to Charlotte's Duke/co-op split and Phoenix's SRP/APS split.

| Program | JEV | Territory |
|---|---:|---|
| Snohomish County PUD — Residential HP | $1,547 | Snohomish County |
| Tacoma Power — Residential HP | $1,238 | Tacoma / Pierce |

Both are marked *Can Participate* — they are real money for the share of the
metro they cover, just not for a Seattle-proper job.

**2 — Different starting fuel or system.** These are open to all incomes but
need a baseline the target customer does not have. A gas furnace disqualifies
every one of them.

| Program | JEV | Needs |
|---|---:|---|
| Seattle Clean Heat — Oil-to-Electric | $1,800 | Oil heat, Mitsubishi equipment |
| PSE — Hybrid (dual-fuel) HP | $844 | Keeps gas backup |
| PSE — Heat pump → heat pump upgrade | $844 | Existing heat pump |
| PSE — Electric resistance → HP | $675 | Electric resistance heat |

**3 — Income-tested.** Ruled out for our customer base.

| Program | JEV | Test |
|---|---:|---|
| WA State HEAR (CCA-funded) | $2,640 | ≤150% AMI |
| PSE — Income-qualified gas → HP | $810 | PSE income table |

## The open questions

**Territory mix.** $600 is the Seattle City Light figure. The card scores a
315K W-TAM metro that spans SCL, PSE, Tacoma Power and Snohomish PUD. A
territory-weighted blend is the right number and we cannot build one without
household shares. Note the direction: PSE is the largest electric territory in
the metro and pays **nothing** on a non-income-qualified gas conversion, so a
true blend may come out **below** $600, not above it.

**Heating-fuel mix.** The card scores gas conversion only, which is Seattle's
thinnest path. Puget Sound carries a large electric-resistance and legacy-oil
stock, and both of those paths pay better. If the trade area is materially
non-gas, the market is being under-scored.

## Enrolment headroom

Ranked by JEV, programs we are not yet enrolled in:

1. **Seattle Clean Heat oil-to-electric — $1,800.** Registration required,
   Mitsubishi equipment only. The $4,000 income-qualified bonus **expires
   30 Sept 2026**; the $2,000 base has no stated end date.
2. **PSE hybrid dual-fuel — $844.** Retains gas backup, so it cuts against the
   all-electric position.
3. **PSE heat pump → heat pump upgrade — $844.**
4. **PSE electric resistance → HP — $675.**

## Not covered here

Heat-pump water heater, EV-charger and weatherization records; the Duvall
pilot (JEV $3,600, but a single-city pilot); and the two federal programs that
have not launched (HEEHRA/Guidehouse, HOMES).
