## How to read it

- **Purchase & Operating Cost (Y):** net purchase price (verified rebate — more rebate = cheaper) weighted 75%, plus operating economics (SPARK) at 25%. Lower = cheaper to own.
- **Operational Complexity (X):** licensing regime — 1 = one statewide license, 5 = every county needs its own exam/application. A local business-license/tax layer adds administrative (not licensing) lift where noted.
- **Rebate reality:** federal HEAR is $0 for non-income-qualified customers; the richest rebates cluster in Northeast programs (NY Clean Heat, NJ Building Decarb). Most Southeast/dual-load markets are thin-rebate, strong-SPARK plays.
