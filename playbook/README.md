# Jetson Expansion Playbook

The single operating manual for launching and scaling Jetson into new markets.

**Audience:** Anyone hired under Brett Zriny (VP of Expansion).

---

### Governance Statement

**This playbook governs how Jetson expands.** It covers everything from market selection through post-launch stabilization. Once a market is stable and formally handed off, it operates under the **GM Playbook**. The Expansion Playbook and GM Playbook are companion documents — expansion gets a market to stable; the GM Playbook governs steady-state operations.
