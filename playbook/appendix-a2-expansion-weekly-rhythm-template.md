# Appendix A-2: Expansion Weekly Rhythm Template
The standard weekly cadence for every Market Launch Lead — predictable, repeatable, and designed to surface risks before they become crises.
## Purpose
Market launches don't fail suddenly. They fail slowly — one missed deadline, one yellow item ignored, one cross-functional handoff that never happened. By the time it's obvious the launch is off track, it's too late to recover without timeline slips or budget overruns.
The weekly rhythm prevents this. It's a set of recurring activities, check-ins, and updates that every Market Launch Lead follows from G0 through G6. It's designed to:
- Surface risks early (yellow items at T-14, not red items at T-1)
- Keep cross-functional partners aligned and unblocked
- Give the VP of Expansion visibility into portfolio health without micromanaging
- Create a predictable communication cadence so stakeholders know when to expect updates
> **Core principle:** Consistency beats heroics. A Market Launch Lead who follows the rhythm will outperform one who works reactively, even if the reactive one works twice as hard.
---
## The Weekly Rhythm (Monday–Friday)
Here's the standard weekly cadence for a Market Launch Lead during an active launch (G0–G5). This rhythm scales whether you're managing one market or supporting multiple.
### Monday
**Morning: Weekly Expansion Launch Call** (60 min)
- **What:** Portfolio-level status review with VP of Expansion and all Market Launch Leads
- **Format:** One-page status update per market (Appendix A-10 template)
- **Output:** Decisions on escalations, resource reallocation, timeline adjustments
- **Your Role:** Present your market's red/yellow/green status, get decisions on help requests
**Afternoon: Week Planning & Smartsheet Update** (90 min)
- Review action items from the launch call
- Update Smartsheet Master Timeline with any decisions (timeline changes, new risks, gate status)
- Identify critical path items at risk this week
- Plan your week: which workstreams need your attention, which cross-functional partners to engage
**End of Day: Partner Sync (Optional, as needed)** (30 min each)
- If you have red or yellow items that need cross-functional support, schedule quick syncs with relevant partners (e.g., Supply Chain, TA, IT, Finance)
- Purpose: unblock issues, clarify next steps, confirm accountability
---
### Tuesday
**Morning: Gate Scorecard Review** (60 min)
- Review the current gate's pass criteria (Appendix A-9)
- Update the gate scorecard: mark each criterion as 🟢/🟡/🔴
- Identify items that are trending from green to yellow (early warning signals)
- Document mitigation plans for yellow and red items
**Afternoon: Cross-Functional Check-Ins** (varies)
- Check in with key workstream owners (examples: Supply Chain on racking permit status, TA on hiring pipeline, IT on systems config)
- This is not a status meeting — it's a "what's blocking you?" conversation
- Update your Smartsheet and gate scorecard based on what you learn
**End of Day: Risk Register Update** (30 min)
- Update your Top 3 Risks list (what could cause timeline slip?)
- For each risk: What's the mitigation plan? Who owns it? What's the status?
- If a new risk emerged this week, add it to the register and flag it for Friday's report
---
### Wednesday
**Morning: Smartsheet Deep Dive** (90 min)
- Review critical path items for the next 2–4 weeks
- Check predecessor dependencies: are upstream tasks on track?
- Update task durations, finish dates, and at-risk flags
- Run a "what-if" scenario: if X slips by 1 week, what's the downstream impact?
**Afternoon: Site or Stakeholder Visit (if applicable)** (varies)
- If you're in pre-launch (G0–G4), visit the warehouse site, meet with the GM, or shadow a cross-functional partner
- If you're post-launch (G5+), visit jobsites, observe installs, or attend the daily ops huddle
- Purpose: Stay close to the ground truth, don't manage from a spreadsheet
**End of Day: Lessons Learned Capture** (15 min)
- Keep a running "lessons learned" log throughout the launch
- Every Wednesday, add 1–2 observations: what worked well, what didn't, what to change for the next launch
- This becomes the foundation for your post-mortem (Appendix A-8)
---
### Thursday
**Morning: Cross-Functional Partner Review (Rotation)** (60 min)
- Each Thursday, schedule a deeper check-in with one cross-functional partner
- Rotation example:
  - Week 1: Supply Chain (racking, inventory, kitting)
  - Week 2: TA / People Ops (hiring pipeline, onboarding)
  - Week 3: IT (systems config, mobile devices, office setup)
  - Week 4: Sales / Marketing (pricing, intake, digital launch)
- Purpose: Go deeper than Tuesday's quick check-ins; review their RACI items for the current gate
**Afternoon: Budget & Spend Review** (30 min)
- Review capex and opex spend to date vs. budget
- Flag any variances (over or under) to Finance and VP of Expansion
- Identify upcoming expenses in the next 2–4 weeks (POs to release, invoices to approve)
**End of Day: Prepare Friday Report** (45 min)
- Draft your one-page status update for the VP of Expansion (see template below)
- This is the weekly summary that keeps the VP informed without needing to micromanage
- Keep it to one page; focus on red/yellow items and decisions needed
---
### Friday
**Morning: Submit Weekly Report to VP of Expansion** (by 10:00 AM)
- Submit your one-page status update (see template below)
- VP of Expansion reviews all reports before the Monday launch call
- If you have urgent escalations, don't wait until Monday — Slack or call the VP of Expansion immediately
**Afternoon: Week Retrospective & Next Week Prep** (60 min)
- Review what you accomplished this week vs. what you planned on Monday
- What went well? What didn't? What blockers emerged?
- Plan next week: critical path items, gate criteria to address, cross-functional dependencies to unblock
- Update your personal task list and calendar for next week
**End of Day: Decompress**
- Market launches are intense. Take time Friday afternoon to step back, avoid burnout, and reset for Monday.
---

## Weekly Rhythm by Launch Phase
The rhythm above is the standard cadence, but the focus shifts depending on which gate you're in:
### G0–G1: Planning & Real Estate (Weeks 1–8)
**Primary Focus:** Market assessment, lease negotiation, budget approval
**Key Activities:**
- Monday: Portfolio launch call (present G0 pass criteria status or G1 real estate progress)
- Tuesday: Gate scorecard review (G0 or G1 criteria)
- Wednesday: Site tours, broker meetings, facility layout planning
- Thursday: Finance check-in (lease terms, budget, insurance)
- Friday: Weekly report (lease negotiation status, timeline to occupancy)
**Common Risks to Track:**
- Lease negotiation dragging
- Site inspection reveals expensive retrofits
- Occupancy date uncertainty
---
### G2–G3: Compliance & Facility Build (Weeks 9–16)
**Primary Focus:** Licensing, leadership hiring, facility setup, ops hiring
**Key Activities:**
- Monday: Portfolio launch call (present G2/G3 gate status)
- Tuesday: Gate scorecard review (compliance and hiring progress)
- Wednesday: Site visit (warehouse setup, racking install progress)
- Thursday: TA check-in (GM/FSM/WM hiring pipeline, ops recruiting)
- Friday: Weekly report (licensing status, hiring pipeline, facility readiness)
**Common Risks to Track:**
- Racking permit delays
- Leadership hiring delays (especially GM)
- Ops hiring shortfall
---
### G4: Physical Readiness & Training (Weeks 17–20)
**Primary Focus:** Fleet delivery, inventory kitting, training certification, sales enablement
**Key Activities:**
- Monday: Portfolio launch call (present G4 pass criteria status)
- Tuesday: Gate scorecard review (12 G4 criteria — this is the longest gate)
- Wednesday: Warehouse visit (kitting progress, mock installs, time trials)
- Thursday: Training check-in (JetStart schedule, certification progress)
- Friday: Weekly report (fleet status, kitting readiness, training certification, sales enablement)
**Common Risks to Track:**
- Vehicle delivery delays (longest lead time item)
- Inventory shortfalls or quality issues
- Training certification failures
---
### G5: Go-Live & Soft Launch (Weeks 21–24)
**Primary Focus:** First installations, daily war room, quality control
**Key Activities:**
- Monday: Portfolio launch call (present G5 status — first install results, volume ramp)
- Tuesday: Join daily ops huddle (with GM, FSM) — shift from weekly to daily cadence
- Wednesday: Jobsite visits (observe installs, talk to customers)
- Thursday: Quality review with FSM (callbacks, QA checklist pass rate, customer NPS)
- Friday: Weekly report (install volume, quality metrics, customer satisfaction, top issues)
**Note:** During G5, the daily launch war room (Section 8.4) becomes the primary coordination mechanism. The weekly rhythm continues but is supplemented by daily standups.
**Common Risks to Track:**
- First install quality issues
- Install time exceeding target
- Customer satisfaction below threshold
---
### G6: Stabilization & Handoff (Weeks 25–36)
**Primary Focus:** Volume ramp, stabilization scorecard, lessons learned, handoff
**Key Activities:**
- Monday: Portfolio launch call (present stabilization scorecard progress)
- Tuesday: Stabilization scorecard review (10 G6 criteria)
- Wednesday: GM partnership meetings (shift from leading to supporting)
- Thursday: Lessons learned documentation (Appendix A-8 template)
- Friday: Weekly report (stabilization metrics, handoff readiness, lessons learned progress)
**Common Risks to Track:**
- Premature handoff (criteria not met)
- GM not ready to take full ownership
- Lessons learned not captured

## Common Rhythm Failures (And the Fix)
| Failure | What Happens | The Fix |
|---------|--------------|---------|
| **Skipping the weekly report** | VP of Expansion loses visibility; can't help when you need it | Friday report is non-negotiable. If you're all green, the report takes 15 minutes. If you're red, the report is how you get help. |
| **Gate scorecard not updated** | You don't realize you're off track until it's too late (yellow becomes red, red becomes crisis) | Tuesday scorecard review is your early warning system. Block 60 min every Tuesday, no exceptions. |
| **Only managing from Smartsheet** | You miss ground truth; issues that aren't in the timeline surprise you | Wednesday site visits keep you connected to reality. Don't manage a launch from a conference room. |
| **Reactive vs. proactive check-ins** | Cross-functional partners don't surface issues until they're blockers | Tuesday check-ins are "what's blocking you?" conversations, not status reports. Make it safe for partners to flag yellow items early. |
| **Working weekends to catch up** | Burnout, mistakes, timeline slips anyway | The rhythm is designed to prevent heroics. If you're working weekends regularly, something is broken — escalate to VP of Expansion. |

## Adapting the Rhythm for Multiple Markets
If you're supporting more than one market simultaneously (future state as Jetson scales), the rhythm adapts:
**Monday:** Present status for all your markets in the portfolio launch call (time-boxed: 10–15 min per market)
**Tuesday–Thursday:** Rotate focus across your markets based on where the highest risks are:
- Market A is at G3 with racking permit issues → prioritize Tuesday/Wednesday for Market A
- Market B is at G1 with lease negotiation going smoothly → lighter touch, just Friday check-in
**Friday:** Submit one report per market (each market gets its own one-page update)
**Principle:** The rhythm scales because it's predictable. Your cross-functional partners know when to expect updates, and you know when to check in with them. You're not reinventing your schedule every week.
---
## If You Only Remember 3 Things
1. **Consistency beats heroics.** Follow the rhythm even when things are going well. It's designed to prevent fires, not just fight them.
2. **Tuesday is your early warning system.** The gate scorecard review surfaces yellow items before they become red. Don't skip it.
3. **Friday's report is your lifeline.** It's how the VP of Expansion knows you need help. If you're stuck, say so. If you're all green, say so. Either way, don't go silent.
