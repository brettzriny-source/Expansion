# Appendix A-4: Expansion RACI Matrix

> The single source of truth for who does what across every phase of a Jetson market launch.

## Purpose

This matrix maps every major launch activity to the person or function responsible for it. No ambiguity. No "I thought you were handling that." Every row has exactly one A — one person who owns the outcome. If you are the A, it ships or it doesn't, and that is on you.

This is a companion to Section 1.2 (RACI Matrix for Market Launch Lifecycle) with full detail across all four launch phases and all cross-functional roles.

> **Core Principle: One A Per Activity. Always.**
> The entire point of a RACI matrix is clarity. If two people think they are the A, nobody is the A. If nobody is marked A, nobody owns the outcome. One A per row — no exceptions. If you are building a new activity and cannot identify a single A, stop and escalate. That is a governance gap, not a staffing problem.

---

## 1. RACI Legend

| **Letter** | **Role** | **What It Means** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R** | Responsible | Does the work. Hands on keyboard, boots on ground. There can be multiple R's per activity. |
| **A** | Accountable | Owns the outcome. Signs off. Only ONE per activity. The buck stops here. |
| **C** | Consulted | Provides input before the work is done. Two-way conversation. Their expertise shapes the decision. |
| **I** | Informed | Kept in the loop after decisions are made. One-way communication. No approval needed from them. |

---

> **Every activity has exactly ONE 'A'. If you see two A's, the matrix is wrong.** Stop. Fix it. Two accountable owners means zero accountable owners. This is the single most common failure mode in cross-functional launches — do not let it happen here.

## 3 Takeaways

**1. One A per row, no exceptions.** Two accountable owners means zero accountable owners. If you cannot name a single A for an activity, you have a governance gap — escalate it.

**2. R without A is just busy work.** The person doing the work needs to know who owns the outcome. If you are the R, find your A. If you are the A, know your R's by name.

**3. The matrix shifts at handoff.** During launch, the VP of Expansion and Market Launch Lead carry most A's. After formal transition, those A's move to the GM and functional leaders. That shift is the entire point of the Expansion-to-GM Playbook handoff.
