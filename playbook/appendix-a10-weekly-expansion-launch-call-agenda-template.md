# Appendix A-10: Weekly Expansion Launch Call Agenda Template
The standardized format for tracking multiple market launches simultaneously with red/yellow/green status indicators for gates and workstreams.
## Purpose
When Jetson is launching 2+ markets simultaneously, the VP of Expansion needs a consistent, efficient way to track progress, identify risks, and allocate resources across the portfolio. This appendix provides the agenda template for the **Weekly Expansion Launch Call** — a standing meeting where all active Market Launch Leads report status, escalate blockers, and get decisions.
This is not a status update meeting for its own sake. It's a **decision-making forum** where the VP of Expansion:
- Identifies cross-market patterns and risks
- Reallocates resources between launches if needed
- Makes greenlight/pause/delay decisions based on gate status
- Unblocks Market Launch Leads who are stuck on cross-functional dependencies
> **Core principle:** One page per market. Red/yellow/green for every gate and workstream. No narrative unless it's attached to a decision request.
---
## Meeting Cadence & Attendance
**Frequency:** Weekly, same day/time (e.g., every Monday 10:00 AM PT)
**Duration:** 60 minutes (scales with number of active markets: ~15 min per market + 15 min buffer for escalations)
**Required Attendees:**
- VP of Expansion (Brett Zriny) — Chair
- Market Launch Leads (one per active market)
**Optional Attendees (Standing Invite):**
- VP of Field Operations (Cameron) — for G4/G5 readiness decisions
- Chief Procurement Officer (Mike Galbraith) — for supply chain/fleet/facility escalations
- VP of Sales (Angie Hirata) — for demand coordination
- VP of Customer Ops (Ryan Delmaire) — for customer ops readiness
- CFO or Finance Lead — for budget/capex escalations
- Head of People/TA — for hiring escalations
**Meeting Owner:** VP of Expansion
**Pre-Work Required:** Each Market Launch Lead submits their one-page status update (template below) **24 hours before the call** via Slack or shared doc

## Agenda Structure
### Part 1: Portfolio Overview (5 minutes)
**Presenter:** VP of Expansion
**Content:**
- Active markets in flight (name, current gate, T-minus status)
- Portfolio-level observations (patterns, resource constraints, timeline trends)
- Agenda for this call (which markets need deep dive vs. quick check-in)
**Example:**
```
Active Markets:
- Philadelphia: G3 → G4 (T-42 to go-live, on track)
- Austin: G1 → G2 (T-78 to go-live, lease negotiation yellow)
- Seattle: G0 → G1 (T-105 to go-live, just greenlighted)
Portfolio Notes:
- Fleet delivery is the bottleneck across all 3 markets (Rivian lead times slipping)
- Talent acquisition is strong (all GM roles filled ahead of schedule)
- Budget variance minimal across portfolio
Today's Focus:
- Austin deep dive (lease negotiation risk)
- Philadelphia quick check (on track, no escalations)
- Seattle quick check (just starting, no issues yet)
```
---
### Part 2: Market-by-Market Status (10–15 minutes per market)
Each market follows the **one-page status template** below. Market Launch Lead presents; VP of Expansion asks clarifying questions and makes decisions.
**Order of Presentation:**
1. Markets with **red items** or **at-risk gates** go first (most time needed)
2. Markets with **yellow items** but no reds go second
3. Markets that are **all green** go last (quick confirmation only)

## Part 3: Cross-Market Patterns & Portfolio Decisions (10 minutes)
**Presenter:** VP of Expansion
**Content:**
After hearing all market updates, the VP of Expansion identifies:
1. **Cross-Market Patterns**
   - Are multiple markets facing the same issue? (e.g., Rivian delivery delays, racking permits, ops hiring shortfalls)
   - Is this a systemic problem that needs a playbook fix or central team intervention?
2. **Resource Reallocation**
   - Should resources (budget, people, vendor capacity) be shifted between markets?
   - Example: "Austin is ahead of schedule; Philadelphia needs ops hiring support. Can we temporarily redirect TA resources?"
3. **Portfolio-Level Decisions**
   - Should any market timeline be adjusted based on portfolio priorities?
   - Should any gate criteria be escalated to executive team for decision?
**Example:**
```
Cross-Market Patterns:
- All 3 markets report Rivian delivery delays (2–3 weeks). This is systemic.
  → Action: Fleet team to escalate with Rivian account manager; consider rental van backup as standard playbook item for future launches.
- Philadelphia and Austin both report racking permit delays.
  → Action: Expansion team to research expedited permitting options and add "permitting strategy" to G1 pass criteria for future markets.
Resource Reallocation:
- Austin is 1 week ahead of schedule on real estate; Philadelphia is 2 weeks behind on ops hiring.
  → Decision: Redirect 50% of TA capacity from Austin to Philadelphia for next 2 weeks.
Portfolio-Level Decisions:
- Philadelphia racking permit delay: **Decision Needed**
  → Decision: Approve temporary racking option ($15K cost) to stay on timeline. Permanent racking swap when permit clears.
```

## How to Use This Template
### For Market Launch Leads
**Before the Call (24 hours prior):**
1. Update your one-page status using this template
2. Mark all gates and workstreams with 🟢/🟡/🔴 status
3. Identify your top 3 risks, top 3 wins, and help needed items
4. Share your status update in the Slack channel or shared doc
**During the Call:**
- Present your status concisely (5–10 minutes)
- Focus on **red and yellow items** — don't spend time on greens unless asked
- Come prepared with **decision requests** and **options** (not just problems)
- If you're all green, say "All green, no escalations" and yield time to other markets
**After the Call:**
- Execute on action items assigned to you
- Update Smartsheet timeline to reflect any decisions made (timeline adjustments, resource changes)
- Communicate decisions to your cross-functional partners
### For the VP of Expansion
**Before the Call:**
- Review all submitted one-page status updates
- Identify which markets need deep dive vs. quick check
- Prepare portfolio-level observations and resource reallocation options
**During the Call:**
- Make decisions on help requests (don't defer unless more data is needed)
- Identify cross-market patterns and systemic issues
- Hold Market Launch Leads accountable for red items (what's the mitigation plan?)
- Reallocate resources if needed (budget, people, vendor capacity)
**After the Call:**
- Document decisions and action items in meeting notes
- Communicate portfolio-level decisions to executive team if needed
- Follow up on action items throughout the week
### For Cross-Functional Partners (Optional Attendees)
- Listen for patterns across markets that affect your function (e.g., TA hears ops hiring shortfalls in 2+ markets)
- Be prepared to commit resources or escalate issues within your team
- Provide input on decision requests that affect your function
- If you own an action item, execute on it and report back to the Market Launch Lead

## Color-Coding Standards
To ensure consistency across all markets, use these definitions:
### Gate Status
- 🟢 **Green:** All pass criteria met OR on track to meet by target date with no significant risks
- 🟡 **Yellow:** 1–2 pass criteria at risk; mitigation plan exists and is being executed; timeline may slip by ≤1 week
- 🔴 **Red:** 3+ pass criteria at risk OR critical blocker with no mitigation plan OR timeline will slip by >1 week
### Workstream Status
- 🟢 **Green:** On track; no significant issues; milestones being hit
- 🟡 **Yellow:** Minor delay or risk; mitigation plan in place; workstream owner confident in recovery
- 🔴 **Red:** Significant delay or blocker; no clear mitigation plan OR workstream owner not confident in recovery OR external dependency blocking progress
### Overall Market Health
- 🟢 **Green:** All gates and workstreams green OR ≤1 yellow workstream; high confidence in go-live date
- 🟡 **Yellow:** 2–4 yellow workstreams OR 1 red workstream; medium confidence in go-live date; timeline may slip by 1–2 weeks
- 🔴 **Red:** 2+ red workstreams OR critical gate blocked; low confidence in go-live date; timeline will slip by >2 weeks OR go-live date should be re-evaluated
### Escalation Rules
- **Green items:** No discussion needed unless Market Launch Lead wants to share a win
- **Yellow items:** Brief mention (1–2 minutes); confirm mitigation plan is working
- **Red items:** Deep dive required (5–10 minutes); VP of Expansion makes decision or escalates further

## If You Only Remember 3 Things
1. **One page per market. Red/yellow/green for every gate and workstream.** No narrative unless it's attached to a decision request. This call is for decisions, not storytelling.
2. **Red and yellow items get airtime; green items get a nod.** If you're all green, say "All green, no escalations" and yield your time. Respect the portfolio — other markets may need help.
3. **Come with options, not just problems.** If you need a decision, present 2–3 options with trade-offs. Make it easy for the VP of Expansion to decide quickly.
